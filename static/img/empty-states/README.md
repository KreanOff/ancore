# Ilustracje pustych stanów

`dashboard-empty.webp` jest dekoracyjną ilustracją wyświetlaną
na pulpitach bez gadżetów oraz w pustych stanach katalogu i zarządzania pulpitami.
Ma rozmiar 1448 × 1086 px i przezroczystość RGBA. Przegląd pulpitu wyświetla ją
do szerokości 440px, a katalog i zarządzanie do 320px.
Powstała 6 października 2026 przez wbudowane narzędzie imagegen,
a PNG przekonwertowano na WebP z zachowaniem kanału alfa (quality=90).

## Prompt użyty do wygenerowania

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a polished colorful empty-dashboard illustration for the center of a Polish productivity app, displayed at about 320px wide.
Primary request: a beautiful multicolored picture communicating that the dashboard is empty and ready for its first widgets.
Subject: a friendly floating blank dashboard panel with a subtle header and three pale empty inset widget slots, surrounded by a few small floating colorful widget tiles. The main panel must clearly feel empty, clean and ready to be filled.
Style/medium: premium soft 3D illustration, tactile matte clay and paper surfaces, gently rounded forms, crisp silhouettes, delicate ambient shadows, refined and cheerful.
Composition: one compact balanced centered composition, slight three-quarter perspective, landscape aspect ratio approximately 4:3, all objects fully visible with generous clear margin.
Color palette: violet and lavender as the main accent to harmonize with the existing purple interface, with tasteful coral, warm yellow, mint and sky blue details. Colorful but calm.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha background, including surrounding margins; no colored backdrop or visible checkerboard.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no people, no screenshot, no entire website layout. The illustration alone. Preserve transparent background.
```

## Puste kolumny gadżetów

`gadget-column-empty.webp` jest dekoracyjną ilustracją pustej kolumny edytora.
Ma rozmiar 720 × 540 px, przezroczystość RGBA i wyświetla się do szerokości 240px.
Powstała 7 października 2026 przez wbudowane narzędzie imagegen z
`dashboard-empty.webp` jako referencją stylu. PNG zmniejszono i zapisano jako
WebP (quality=90, method=6), zachowując kanał alfa.

Prompt użyty do wygenerowania:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a compact decorative illustration for the empty gadget columns of a Polish productivity dashboard editor, displayed at 200px wide.
Input image: the attached dashboard-empty illustration is a STYLE REFERENCE. Create a new companion asset, keep the same premium soft 3D clay finish, rounded forms, gentle lighting and cheerful pastel colors.
Primary request: a beautiful multicolored illustration inviting the user to add or drag a widget into an empty column.
Subject: a small floating ivory and lavender widget tray with one clearly empty inset slot, a coral rounded statistics tile hovering just above it ready to be placed, and two small mint and sky-blue widget tiles beside the tray. A small warm yellow sparkle gives a welcoming detail.
Composition: one compact centered balanced composition, slight three-quarter perspective, landscape 4:3 proportions, all elements fully visible with generous transparent margin. Bold clean silhouettes that read well at small sizes.
Color palette: lavender and violet, coral pink, mint green, sky blue and warm yellow, matching the reference illustration.
Materials and lighting: tactile matte clay, soft rounded edges, subtle ambient contact shadows, refined high quality 3D render.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha background, including all surrounding margins.
Constraints: illustration only; no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no people, no screenshot, no entire website layout, no visible checkerboard.
```

## Pusty katalog przestrzeni

`spaces-empty.webp` jest dekoracyjną ilustracją pustego katalogu i zarządzania
przestrzeniami. Ma rozmiar 720 × 540 px, przezroczystość RGBA i wyświetla się
maksymalnie w szerokości 320px. Powstała 7 października 2026 przez wbudowane
narzędzie imagegen z `dashboard-empty.webp` jako referencją stylu. PNG zmniejszono
i zapisano jako WebP (quality=90, method=6), zachowując kanał alfa.

Prompt użyty do wygenerowania:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: decorative empty-state illustration for the Spaces directory of a Polish teamwork and task-management app, displayed at 320px wide.
Input image: dashboard-empty.webp is a STYLE REFERENCE. Make a new companion illustration for Spaces using the same premium soft 3D clay finish, soft rounded forms, cheerful pastel colors and gentle studio lighting.
Primary request: a beautiful multicolored picture showing a workspace ready for a team's first project.
Subject: three floating overlapping rounded project panels arranged like a small stack of layers. The rear panel is lavender, the middle panel is sky blue, and the front panel is warm ivory with three simple tiny blank task cards in mint, coral pink and warm yellow. A small lavender plus tile and a few restrained colored spheres suggest creating a new space. Clear simple shapes, no text.
Composition: compact balanced centered composition with slight three-quarter perspective, landscape 4:3 proportions, clean strong silhouettes that read well at small display sizes, every object fully visible with generous transparent margins.
Materials and lighting: tactile matte clay and soft paper surfaces, gently beveled edges, subtle ambient shadows, crisp polished 3D illustration.
Color palette: lavender and violet, sky blue, mint green, coral pink and warm yellow, harmonious with the reference.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha background, including the surrounding margins.
Constraints: the illustration alone; no text, letters, numbers, logos, people, screenshots, entire website layouts, visible checkerboards or opaque background.
```
